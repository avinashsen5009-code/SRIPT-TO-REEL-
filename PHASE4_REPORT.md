# Phase 4 — grounded metaphor props (review gate)

**Added:** `metaphor-props.js` is a local, deterministic Canvas vocabulary of 23 distinct vector props: iceberg, seed/tree, mountain/flag, snapping rope, hourglass, flame/streak, brain/gears, battery, anchor, maze/exit, mirror, key/lock, compass, dumbbell, candle, ladder, scale, domino chain, lightbulb, book stack, graduation cap, calendar, clock. Story and Explainer share the registry (89 registered components in total); no framework, image API, stock service, generated statistics, or network call was added for these props. The palette remains restrained in each style. The 16:9 companion panel now depicts the same spoken prop instead of an unrelated generic ring.

**Selection and source limits:** A prop is proposed only when an English, Hindi or selected Roman-Hinglish literal noun/phrase is found as a whole concept. The matcher checks action cues separately: an idle prop does not snap, grow, tilt, open, light or mark until the relevant action has been spoken. Explicit percentages, short day counts and clock-hour cues are source-bound and gated by their word onset; missing numbers remain absent. Unknown topics fall back to existing typography/other deliberate treatments. Existing educational components, explicit graphs, manual treatments, pinned visuals and selected user media have precedence. A scene with a matched prop has a compact **Metaphor prop · review** disclosure with **Auto / Use this spoken prop / Use existing treatment**; the choice is saved in the existing project scene settings. Manually chosen graph and educational treatments are not replaced.

**Fixed:** Legacy icon matching no longer uses loose prefixes that mapped `seed` to `see` (eye), `snapping` to `app` (phone), or `chain` to `chai` (tea). Explainer's fallback symbol matching is now word-boundary based; unmatched narration uses typography, not a guessed object. Existing icon library and visual picker remain available.

**Tests performed:** `node --check` on modified modules; `npm test` passed, including all 23 prop IDs in both skins and at three timestamps, 23 source-match cases, unknown/faux-match negatives, action-only behavior, prop control/persistence, and chart/explicit-graph precedence. Headless Chromium at a 390px viewport rendered 23 curated sentences in Story and Explainer at an early and late timestamp (**92 actual frames**); their contact sheet was visually reviewed. All 46 style/scene result records matched the intended prop; all 23 auto selections were active on this *curated* set. No browser page exceptions or horizontal document overflow were observed. A separate Chromium integration loaded a silent WAV, verified the saved per-scene choice and the corrected false icon matches, rendered 720×1280 portrait and 1280×720 landscape, and exported a nonempty portrait MP4 (218,893 bytes) with no page exception. These are actual tests, not evidence of production reliability.

**Known limitations:** Matching is a small hand-authored lexicon, not full language understanding: inflected and code-switched narration outside the included cues can be missed, and an explicit metaphor word can still be inappropriate in context. The simplified pictures are illustrative, not factual maps, anatomy or scientific/financial evidence. The hourglass and calendar do not measure real elapsed time; no unspecified dates or exact battery levels are fabricated. Captions/animation depend on estimated or edited word onsets; the browser tests used synthetic/silent audio, not real mixed-language speech. No physical-phone performance measurement, audible export check, cross-browser/codec matrix, arbitrary narration guarantee, or formal comprehension study was performed. The landscape composition intentionally retains the existing split layout and may need scene-specific recropping. Optional first-use speech model download/cache and legacy dependencies retain their earlier limitations.
