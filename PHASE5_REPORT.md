# Phase 5 — test, clarity review and mobile polish

## What changed
- Expanded **Captions → Position** from 55–62% to **45–82%** of portrait height and **Size** from 70–140% to **40–140%**. The Story, Sketch/Trending/Classic, Structural, and Explainer render paths use the stored size/position; the landscape caption compositor moves the caption on the right-hand panel too. The saved-project loader accepts the wider ranges. An inline warning tells users to check safe zones when they put captions near the bottom; 82% can overlap a social platform's controls. Existing captions, highlight/style selectors and tap-to-edit remain.
- Cached the waveform's static bars, bands, scene labels and cut marks, redrawing only the playhead during playback. Cache invalidates when audio peaks, width, cuts, theme colours or scene count change. The mobile caption sliders now have a touch-friendly height; existing panel hierarchy is unchanged.
- Fixed a real visual failure uncovered in the Hindi chart review: the educational parser's token index had counted Devanagari combining marks incorrectly. A two-value Hindi bar chart appeared empty near the end of a scene. Hindi tokens now include combining marks, numeric chart values reveal only from their own spoken word, and a late final word finishes its reveal within the scene. A before/after chart snapshot and a regression assertion verified the second value is not shown early.

## Tests actually performed
1. **45 curated sentences**: 15 English, 15 Devanagari Hindi and 15 Roman Hinglish; within each language, five self-improvement, five educational and five source-gated prop scenes. Both Story and Explainer rendered each scene in Chromium at its last moment: **90 actual frames**, zero expected-component mismatches, zero browser page exceptions and no document overflow at a 390px viewport. [Contact sheet](../phase5-contact-sheet.png), [45-scene clarity records](../phase5-clarity.json), [source sentences](../phase5-samples.json).
2. **Clarity report**: every scene records its idea, source-driven node count, nominal component font floor, static graph-palette contrast estimate, graph-linter overlap status, source word-index reveal order, active treatment and graph-linter issues. In these 45 fixtures the linter reported no graph issues/overlap; the nominal font floor was 32px for self/educational and 38px for props, and the graph palette estimate was 17.75:1. **These are not measurements of glyph pixels, text-box collisions or the selected component's actual contrast.** Contact-sheet inspection found the Hindi chart defect described above; it was corrected and the sheet regenerated.
3. **All component types**: a separate direct Canvas-render smoke test invoked **all 89 registered Explainer components** with a source fixture or a generic renderer fixture; 89/89 completed without a thrown exception or page error. This is distinct from the 45-scene editor contact sheet and is **not** a claim that every component is semantically correct or visually collision-free. [Direct-render results](../phase5-component-coverage.json).
4. **Syntax and smoke**: `node --check` passed for every root-level `.js`/`.cjs` plus the combined inline scripts in `index.html`; `npm test` passed, including the Hindi chart's early/late numeric gate and caption-setting save/reopen clamps.
5. **Mobile/audio/export**: headless Chromium loaded a sample silent WAV through the actual upload path, set 40% caption size and 82% position, reopened a saved project and verified the settings and caption hit targets. No page errors or horizontal overflow occurred at 390px; a separate 320px viewport had no horizontal overflow and verified waveform cache reuse/cut invalidation. Both 720×1280 and 1280×720 paths rendered; short portrait and landscape exports were nonempty MP4 files with an `ftyp` brand. Details: [integration JSON](../phase5-integration.json), [portrait screenshot](../phase5-mobile-small-captions.png), [landscape screenshot](../phase5-wide-small-captions.png).

## Not tested / limitations
The WAV is silent; this work does not verify recognition/alignment on real Hindi/Hinglish narration, audible MP4 output, playback on a physical phone, actual frame-time gains, battery consumption, browser/codec coverage, accessibility with every assistive technology, correct visuals for arbitrary narration, or comprehension by human viewers. The scene-graph overlap and contrast numbers are static approximations, **not measured collisions or actual contrast for educational/prop artwork**. Small bottom captions may be hard to read or covered by a platform interface; check the preview, safe zones and exported clip on the destination device. Optional first-use speech-model downloads still require network access and browser storage; the ZIP does not bundle model weights or promise reliable offline transcription.
