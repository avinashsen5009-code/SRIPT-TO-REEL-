# Phase 3 — explainer and educational components (review gate)

**Added:** `educational-components.js` registers 22 additional components through the existing `ExplainerStyle.components` map, shared by the Story and Explainer drawing paths: AnalogyCard, VennDiagram, ConceptMap, MindMap, DecisionTree, BarChart, LineChart, PieOrDonut, Matrix2x2, history Timeline, NumberLine, FormulaBuilder, MythFactFlip, TrueFalse, QuizThreeOption, FlashcardFlip, MnemonicLetters, schematic MapPin, schematic AnatomyDiagram, ice→water StateChange, schematic CrossSection and numeric ScaleCompare. All require recognisable, explicitly spoken inputs. Source-bound amounts, years, optional answer keys and word indices stay separate from manually editable short labels. Educational scenes get a compact optional inspect/edit/disable control; edits persist in saved browser projects. Story and Explainer remain user-selectable, and Auto mix still chooses between them.

**Fixed:** Charts show only explicitly paired labels and nonnegative numerical values actually spoken; no series or zero bars are inserted while later words have not been spoken. A quiz reveals a correct-option highlight **only after the answer token is spoken**, and an omitted answer leaves all options unmarked. Myth text is visibly marked `MYTH`, followed by `FACT` only at the supplied correction. Long labels wrap to two lines rather than being uniformly horizontally squeezed. Changing a chart label must retain its spoken amount; number-line values are locked to the script. Unknown places are shown as **SCHEMATIC, not a map coordinate**. The simple anatomy and cross-section visuals state they are schematic and not to scale. State-change animation is limited to explicit ice→water, not a claimed depiction of arbitrary chemistry.

**Skipped (already existed / reused instead of duplicates):** General DefinitionCard uses `scene-graph.js:fromText/render`; LabeledDiagram and multi-node relation graphs use `visual-grammar.js:render` and `scene-graph.js:render`; StepFlow, CauseEffectChain, generic Cycle and Pipeline reuse existing graph/FlowChips functionality, with their existing limitations. Spoken single-value counters use `explainer-style.js:CounterCard` and Phase 2 StatCounter. KeyTakeaway/Remember visuals use existing statement/caption rendering, and ordinary recap lists use the existing ListCard/scene graph. Preview, portrait/wide captions, media, saved projects, SRT and MP4 export remain unchanged. No paid services, new framework or build step.

**Tested:** `node --check` on all first-party JS/CJS plus the inline index script; `npm test` with 22-ID registry, source-gated chart/quiz/year/schematic assertions. Chromium rendered **45 curated sentences (15 English, 15 Devanagari Hindi, 15 Roman Hinglish) in both Story and Explainer** (90 actual editor frames) with zero page exceptions and zero expected-treatment mismatches on that set. The contact sheet was visually reviewed. A silent three-second WAV was loaded/decoded; the existing export path produced nonempty portrait 720×1280 MP4 (~963 kB) and wide 1280×720 MP4 (~707 kB). At 390px viewport there was no horizontal page overflow, and repeated rendering at the same timestamp produced identical frame data. A manually edited educational label survived saved-project reopen; turning the treatment off disabled it. A quiz's correct option was white before its answer word and highlighted only after it, verified from pixels in Chromium. `../phase3-clarity.json` contains a nominal 45-scene report (idea, label count, declared font floor, palette contrast, word-index reveal order, review flags).

**Not tested:** Recognition/alignment with real speech, audible exported audio, playback of the MP4s on a physical phone, all codecs/browsers, factual accuracy of user-provided data, correctness for arbitrary Hindi/Hinglish/English sentences, formal comprehension testing, true measured text-box collisions or a real mid-range-phone <12-ms frame time. The clarity file expressly marks overlap as *not pixel-measured*.

**Known limitations:** MapPin does not plot a real geographic coordinate; the diagram is a schematic pointer only. Anatomy and cross-section illustrations are not medically or scientifically precise drawings, and do not supply unsaid labels or claims. Pie and line visuals scale **only** sourced nonnegative numbers; negative-value data does not auto-select a chart, and pie sectors represent relative shares rather than an invented 100%-claim. Historical timelines label supplied years/events; their visual spacing is not a to-scale chronology. Venn overlap requires a shared property in the script. The option/correction reveal depends on word timing quality. Mnemonic expansions may need manual review if their first letters do not match. Formula parsing is intentionally conservative. Reused generic graph flows do not yet solve every education sentence; the Phase 1 linter is still not pixel-perfect, and 8-second interrupts are not enforced. The known legacy icon-vocabulary false matches remain for Phase 4; new schematic graphics do not use the old icon lookup.
